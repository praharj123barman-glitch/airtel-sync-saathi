ACCEL BUSINESS · SAATHI · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
(Prototype knowledge base for the Airtel SYNC 2026 case. Accel Telecom is fictional and the data is synthetic.)

Q: What is Saathi?
A: Saathi is Accel Business's digital advisor for small businesses. In one chat it suggests the right bundle,
shows one monthly price, verifies your business and lets you track your order. A real person is always one tap away.

Q: Which languages does Saathi speak?
A: Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Bengali and Punjabi, by chat or voice.

Q: What can I buy?
A: Business fibre (100 Mbps, 300 Mbps, 1 Gbps), 5G AirFiber, leased lines, business postpaid SIMs, 4G/5G failover,
static IP, SD-WAN, cloud telephony, toll-free numbers, business SMS, WhatsApp Business API, cloud email and office
suite, cloud backup, website + domain, cloud POS and billing, endpoint security, managed firewall, secure Wi-Fi,
cloud CCTV, machine-monitoring IoT and fleet trackers. Everything is on one bill.

Q: How long does installation take?
A: 5G AirFiber about 2 days; business fibre about 5 days (1 Gbps about 7); business SIMs 1 day; cloud services 1–3
days; CCTV about 4 days; leased line 20 Mbps about 21 days and 100 Mbps about 30 days; SD-WAN about 14 days. The
quote shows the expected time for your bundle.

Q: What is the activation guarantee? Do I get connected in 72 hours?
A: Every quote shows one guarantee, and that is the one we keep. Small single-site businesses in tier-1 cities on
business fibre or 5G AirFiber see "Connected in 72 hours, guaranteed": if fibre takes longer, we connect you on 5G
AirFiber first and switch you over when the fibre is installed. Everyone else sees a firm number of days, for
example "Connected within 10 days of your order, guaranteed", based on how long past orders like yours took. The
exact rules are under ACTIVATION PROMISE in the bundles and rules document. Either way, if we miss it, month one is
free: the whole first monthly bill is credited automatically. You don't need to ask. Saathi messages you at every
stage (order confirmed, business verified, installer booked, connected), so you never have to chase us. (The other
items in a bundle, like CCTV or software, follow the install time shown in the quote.)
Saathi never offers 72 hours when your quote shows a number of days: the quote decides.

Q: What happens if my internet goes down?
A: You're credited automatically, usually before you even contact us. Outages under 60 minutes get no credit (we
still tell you). Beyond that, each started 4 hours of downtime credits one day's bill (the monthly bill divided by
30). If your bundle has 4G/5G failover, you stayed online, so the credit is half. The credit is capped at half of
the monthly bill. Saathi messages you with the amount; it appears on your next bill.

Q: What if fibre isn't available at my shop?
A: You get Biz 5G AirFiber: plug-and-play wireless broadband, live in about 2 days, no digging. You can move to fibre
later.

Q: Is GST included in the price?
A: No, GST is extra. Setup fees are one-time and shown separately.

Q: How long is the contract?
A: It depends on the items: 6 months for 5G AirFiber, 12 months for most bundles, 24 months for leased line 100 Mbps,
SD-WAN, managed firewall and machine IoT. The quote always shows the plan length.

Q: I don't have a GST registration. Can I still buy?
A: Yes. Fibre, AirFiber, business SIMs, failover, static IP, cloud email, backup, website, POS, security software,
Wi-Fi and CCTV don't need GST. Products that need an active GSTIN: Biz Fibre 1G, leased lines, SD-WAN, cloud
telephony, toll-free, business SMS, WhatsApp Business API, managed firewall and machine IoT.

Q: Which documents do I need?
A: Usually none to upload. If you have a GSTIN, Saathi checks it in the GST registry directly, so no GST certificate
is needed. Udyam is optional. The signatory's ID comes from DigiLocker and is signed with Aadhaar e-Sign. An address
proof dated within the last 90 days is needed only if you have no GSTIN or the installation address differs from
the GST address.

Q: The GSTIN lookup didn't work. What now?
A: Take a photo of your GST registration certificate. Saathi reads it on your phone (the photo isn't uploaded),
shows you the GSTIN, name and address it read so you can correct them, and then checks the GSTIN exactly as if you
had typed it. This needs your yes to KYC verification. If anything doesn't match the registry, a specialist checks
it with you.

Q: What did I agree to, and how do I take it back?
A: Saathi asks separately about three things: looking up your registrations, verifying your business (KYC), and
offers on WhatsApp. Say "withdraw consent" any time and tell Saathi which one; it stops right away and Saathi
tells you what that changes. Saying no to offers never affects your plan.

Q: Why was my GSTIN not accepted?
A: Either it has a typo (the last character is a checksum) or the GST registry shows it as Cancelled or Suspended.
GST-only products can't be activated on a cancelled or suspended GSTIN. You can continue with a plan that doesn't
need GST, or talk to our team.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You shouldn't need to: Saathi sends a WhatsApp update at each step. You can always ask Saathi "where is my order"
and share your order number (it looks like UD-0068); it shows the timeline and the hours left until the time
guaranteed on your quote. (In this prototype the orders are demo orders and the WhatsApp updates are shown in the tracker, not sent.)

Q: Can I change my bundle?
A: Yes. Before you accept, add or remove anything and the price updates instantly. After activation, ask Saathi or
your account specialist.

Q: Who installs it?
A: An Accel engineer or a certified local Accel partner. You choose the visit slot and can reschedule in the chat.

Q: How do I pay?
A: The first payment and monthly bills can be paid by UPI Autopay. You get one bill for everything. (In this
prototype, payment and UPI Autopay are simulated: no money is taken.)

Q: I have 3 or more branches or a factory. Can Saathi help?
A: Yes. Saathi prepares your quote and verifies your business, then a specialist helps with site surveys,
leased lines or SD-WAN.

Q: Can I talk to a person?
A: Any time. Tap "Talk to a person", share your number and a good time, and a specialist or local partner will call.

Q: Are there discounts?
A: The quote already shows the best catalogue price for your plan length. Saathi does not offer other discounts. A
specialist can discuss larger orders.
