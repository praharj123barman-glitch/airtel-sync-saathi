HOW PRICES WORK
- One monthly price for the whole bundle, on one bill, with one support number.
- Prices shown are the catalogue price for the committed plan length. GST is extra.
- One-time setup fees apply only to some items (for example failover router, POS, CCTV, leased line). They are
  shown separately on the quote.
- Plan length = the longest minimum term among the items: 6 months for 5G AirFiber, 12 months for fibre and most
  services, 24 months for leased line 100 Mbps, SD-WAN, managed firewall and machine IoT. Business postpaid lines
  have a 1-month minimum.
- Installation time = the slowest item in the bundle: 5G AirFiber about 2 days, business fibre about 5 days
  (1 Gbps about 7), leased line about 21–30 days, SD-WAN about 14 days, managed firewall about 10 days.

THE FOUR BUNDLES (who each is for and how it is sold; the exact contents, quantities, prices and worked examples are
in the generated section right after this one)

1) DUKAAN DIGITAL · for micro retail and services (kirana, salon, pharmacy, bakery, boutique, optician)
   A business connection (fibre, or plug-and-play 5G AirFiber where fibre doesn't reach) and business mobile lines.
   Popular add-ons: billing (Cloud POS), CCTV, secure Wi-Fi with a guest network, a website, WhatsApp Business API.
   Sold mainly self-serve on WhatsApp, with local partners helping in-store.

2) OFFICE PRO · for professional offices (clinic, diagnostic lab, CA firm, law office, coaching centre, agency)
   Fibre sized to the team, business lines, cloud email, device security and cloud backup, with 4G/5G failover for
   bigger teams or anyone who needs uptime. Add-ons: cloud telephony, static IP, SMS packs, toll-free.

3) CHAIN CONNECT · for small multi-site businesses (restaurant, pharmacy, apparel and gym chains)
   The same kit at every site (connection, failover, secure Wi-Fi, and billing for retail and food), plus business
   lines and a toll-free number. Optional Managed SD-WAN and CCTV. A specialist joins after Saathi qualifies the need.

4) FACTORY ASSURED · for small manufacturers (auto parts, textiles, packaging, food processing)
   A leased line with an uptime SLA and a managed firewall (fibre with failover instead when a leased line isn't
   possible), business lines and cloud email. Machine-monitoring sensors when they want to track machines.
   Field-led sale (site survey), with Saathi doing the quote, KYC and tracking.

  CCTV, cloud telephony, SD-WAN, managed firewall.

NOT SURE / TOO EXPENSIVE?

  exactly what changed.
